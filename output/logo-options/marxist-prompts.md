## 04-workers-book

Use case: logo-brand. Design a distinctive logo exploration for "Praxis", a specifically MARXIST reading and study iOS app. Exact brand text "Praxis", capital P and lowercase raxis. Its name means theory joined to practice. The political identity MUST be immediately apparent, not merely a generic library symbol colored red. Take visual language from radical workers' publishing and disciplined early twentieth-century political print design. Create an original graphic emblem, with a robust silhouette and considered negative spaces. Clean flat vector-like art, solid vermilion red #C3261F, warm ivory #F5EEDC, near-black #171512. Deliberate strong typography with individual character. Present on a spacious landscape ivory sheet: one large symbol plus Praxis wordmark, and a smaller app icon application on the right using exactly the SAME emblem. Only text is "Praxis". All artwork flat solid colors with no texture, no shading, no gradients, no 3D, no glows. Avoid generic book clip art, bank/insurance identity, startup swooshes, AI sparkles, infinity loops, decorative filler, explanatory captions and slogans. This should feel like a real political publisher commissioned a graphic designer.
Direction: THE WORKERS' BOOK. Make a compact bold emblem that integrates the explicitly Marxist HAMMER AND SICKLE with an open book. One decisive sickle curve and a thick straight hammer, with the book represented by just two angular planes forming a solid base. Design these as ONE coherent silhouette, not three icons pasted together. Readability and original negative-space construction matter. No star, no circle, no badge rim. Solid red primary emblem and strong sober condensed sans-serif Praxis wordmark. App icon: red emblem on near-black square, generous margin. The political emblem must be recognizable and proportioned with care.
