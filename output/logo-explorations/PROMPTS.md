No stars, flags, weapons, portraits or added symbols. Make the hand anatomically readable and book unmistakable. Large shapes and open counters, readable at app icon sizes.

Crisp flat vector-like edges. Red, near-black and warm cream only. Avoid gradients, drop shadows, distressed texture, pseudo-Cyrillic letter substitutions and ornamental clutter. A contemporary logo informed by historical design, not a whole propaganda poster.
