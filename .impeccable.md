## Design Context

### Users
People using the Leitor Marxista / Marxist Reader apps to discover, save, and read texts from the Marxists Internet Archive, often in longer reading sessions.

### Brand Personality
Independent, thoughtful, and reader-focused. The interface should feel clean, but polished, making a large public archive feel approachable and trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Modern editorial reading interface: clean hierarchy and generous breathing room, finished with precise type, spacing, and interaction details. Use the Mobbin references from Fable and ElevenReader for clear library organization, visible reading progress, and accessible reader controls. Keep the existing red identity as an accent, with quiet paper-like surfaces in the library and a low-distraction reading surface. Preserve the reader's light, sepia, grey, dark, black, and custom themes. Aim for a polished MVP that feels complete without adding decorative complexity.

### Design Principles
- Make it easy to resume a text and see reading progress.
- Organize saved and recent texts with clear hierarchy and lightweight separators.
- Keep reading typography calm, configurable, and comfortable for long sessions.
- Preserve support for Portuguese and English, light and dark themes, and accessible touch targets.
- Keep archive discovery, saved items, and the source attribution visible without competing with the text.
